Traxettle is an expense splitter and shared expense tracker built for trips, roommates, events, group outings, and everyday bill splitting.

If you need an easy way to split bills, manage group expenses, track who paid, and settle up fairly, Traxettle helps you do it without spreadsheets, chat confusion, or manual calculations.

WHY PEOPLE USE TRAXETTLE
• Split expenses for trips, vacations, dinners, weddings, parties, and shared living
• Collect money up front in a shared pool and spend from it
• Track group spending in one place
• See who owes whom with clear settlement flows
• Manage shared expenses across multiple people and multiple currencies
• Keep a clean history of active, settled, and closed events

KEY FEATURES
• Expense splitting app for groups, friends, couples, families, and roommates
• Smart bill split options: equal, ratio, exact amounts, or paid on behalf of others
• Event-based expense tracking so every trip or outing stays organized
• A date on every expense, and a filter to hide private expenses
• Shared expense manager with participant-level visibility
• Easy settlement tracking with payer and payee clarity
• Multi-currency expense splitting for international travel and cross-border groups
• Receipts on any expense, from the camera or your photos
• Pool money: collect first, spend from the pool, and settle up at the end
• Invitations so people can join events and contribute expenses
• Closed event history for records and reference
• CSV and PDF exports for expense reports and reconciliation
• No ads during your expense management workflow
